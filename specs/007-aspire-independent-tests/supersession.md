# Prospective supersession of historical testing agreements

**Date**: 2026-10-16

**Original authority (2026-10-16; amended below)**: The human owner confirmed the scope recorded in [the feature specification](spec.md), including removal of all automated Aspire/AppHost tests and publishing coverage, preservation of distinct application/provider guarantees, manual local Aspire verification, consolidation into Backend.Tests, and deferred parallelization. This approval authorizes the policy and implementation work; it is not final acceptance of feature 007.

## Original policy and evidence (as accepted 2026-10-16)

From feature 007 onward, required automated application checks do not construct, inspect, start, command, or publish an AppHost. Retained backend integration checks use service factories and real isolated infrastructure containers. Recovery evidence uses service-host recreation and real container restarts against the same durable state rather than separate application processes or multiple AppHosts. The Smoke.Tests project is retired after its retained guarantees have replacements.

The [coverage disposition](team/coverage-disposition.md) is the authoritative source-method/theory-case and mixed-assertion mapping to final executable replacements. This register identifies affected historical agreements; it does not substitute for their coverage mapping or claim that planned replacements have passed. Current validation, independent reviews, convergence, and separate human acceptance belong to feature 007's team records.

Local Aspire startup, endpoint allocation, dashboard links, console configuration/access, effective loopback bindings, and local lifecycle wiring are checked manually using [the current quickstart](quickstart.md) and the contributor guidance. Their local behavior remains supported. Manifest generation, publication, deployment output, and cloud deployment are outside the supported scope: historical output-exclusion assertions and obligations are superseded, rather than replaced by a manual manifest check. Existing run/development guards and local runtime configuration are preserved; this policy does not authorize changing production source or weakening provider/application security.

Browser behavior and accessibility gates remain applicable. Telemetry still uses production registration, public behavior, real provider contracts, correlated exports, privacy checks, and existing mutation-sensitivity checks where required. Representative dashboard inspection is manual; removing automated AppHost coverage does not remove those telemetry guarantees. Missing infrastructure prerequisites and unavailable required observations remain failures or pending evidence, never successful skips. No new test concurrency policy is introduced.

## Preservation and interpretation

Prospective notices are appended to each historical spec, plan and tasks file. Text before those notices, accepted status, completed task markers, review references, hashes and acceptance dates are preserved. Historical team reports and verdicts are not edited. The notices identify a later owner-approved policy change; they do not retroactively invalidate truthful old results or claim old reviewers approved the new harness.

Older quickstarts, research, contracts and team evidence remain historical context when they describe superseded test paths or manifest workflows. For current test commands and local-development checks, follow feature 007 and current contributor guidance. Runtime/wire/security contracts remain required unless explicitly superseded above; this record authorizes no data deletion, credential reset, source-runtime change, new deployment support or concurrency optimization.

## Testing organization and concurrency amendment — 2026-10-19

**Authority**: subsequent explicit owner instructions in the implementation conversation: “let's do a v3 migration and try to leverage `ParallelMode.All`”; “Let's adopt it” for Microsoft.Testing.Platform; the selected nine-project plus TestSupport layout; “allow all projects to run in parallel” and full parallelization within projects; and parallel-by-default engineering guidance with sequential execution only for hard documented constraints. The current request authorizes reconciliation of these changes with Spec Kit artifacts. These are implementation/scope authorizations, not final acceptance or independent reviewer verdicts.

**Superseded**: feature 007's original FR-006 single Backend.Tests location and FR-011 no-concurrency requirement, corresponding SC-006 wording, original plan dependency/layout/scheduling decisions, original tasks' scope constraints, and the research decision to retain assembly serialization. The accepted original task markers, checklists, source freezes, hashes and review reports retain their historical meaning. Features 001–006 already refer here through their prospective supersession notices; their old Backend.Tests/Smoke paths are historical locations, not instructions to recreate those projects. Current executable locations are listed in the coverage-disposition addendum.

**Current policy**: nine responsibility-based backend test projects plus non-test TestSupport; xUnit v3 via `xunit.v3.mtp-v2` 4.0.2; native MTP `dotnet test`; all modules permitted concurrently and `ParallelMode.All` / `MaxThreads = -1` within projects. Sequential collections/classes require hard documented interference constraints and the smallest justified scope, per [engineering policy](../../docs/engineering/testing.md#backend-parallelism-is-the-default). Scenario isolation and concurrency-safe fixtures support this policy. Shared containers alone are not grounds for exclusion. Application/runtime, real-provider fidelity, security/privacy, browser checks and the no-automated-AppHost/manual-local boundary remain in force. Constitution 1.1.0 remains unchanged.

**Evidence and remaining gates**: [validation addendum](team/validation.md#testing-amendment-validation--2026-10-19), [current coverage locations](team/coverage-disposition.md#current-executable-locations--2026-10-19), T023–T027, and `team/run-state.json` distinguish implemented/automatically validated changes from pending independent quality/security review, convergence and explicit acceptance. The original acceptance and reviewer verdicts do not extend to this amendment.
